# Memberly
I want to build an iPhone app called Memberly that is able to scan and take in photos or screenshots of membership IDs and codes and numbers and store them in an online wallet of sorts, so that instead of needing to bring all these physical cards or pull up all these different websites that have my membership accounts linked to them, I can simply upload them to the Memberly app and store them in a wallet. Then, when I go somewhere, I can open my membership card in that wallet and scan in, and this way it can take in and compile all of these different programs I'm a part of. I want the first screen to be a mix of my current uploaded cards, with a plus button or an option to add more of them. If you click on "add more," then the screen should pull up the question of "add from camera roll" or "take photo." If I say "add from camera roll," it pulls up my camera roll. I can select a photo, upload it. It'll scan, show it scanning. It'll create a card that has the mock-up to double-check. That the details are correct. If they are correct, I can hit Approve and Add to Wallet. If I say Take Picture, then it will pull up the camera and an outline for where I should place the card, so that way I can take a photo and it can scan straight from that. Then it should be the same confirmed details and Add to Wallet. I want also maybe a third screen that shows a map with where my current location is and the local programs nearby in case I want to register or add something. I want this to be run through expo go.
